About Lovely Professional University (LPU)

Lovely Professional University (LPU) is a premier private university located in Chaheru, Phagwara, Punjab, India. Established in 2005, it has rapidly grown into one of the largest single-campus universities in the country, known for its sprawling 600+ acre ultra-modern infrastructure and vibrant, multicultural campus life.

LPU is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and holds top rankings from the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) across various disciplines, including Engineering, Management, and Pharmacy. The university acts as a global education hub, hosting thousands of students from all Indian states and over 50 countries worldwide.

Key Highlights of LPU:

  • World-Class Infrastructure: Features a massive campus with high-tech labs, a Mac lab, an indoor sports stadium, an Olympic-size swimming pool, and an in-campus mall.
  • Industry-Driven Education: Offers tech-focused and practical curriculum tied up with global giants like Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and Bosch.
  • Stellar Placements: Known for its strong placement record, with alumni working in top Fortune 500 companies globally.
  • Vibrant Campus Culture: Hosts massive national and international youth festivals, cultural events, and tech expos throughout the year.
